Essential Nutrition During Chemotherapy

Nutritional wellness during active oncological treatments plays an instrumental role in sustaining blood counts, repairing healthy tissues, and curbing nausea.

Hydration & Electrolytes

Consume room-temperature coconut water, thin dal water, and lemon juice in frequent small sips.

Gentle Protein Intake

Sattu water, thoroughly boiled eggs, boiled moong dal, and soft paneer aid tissue healing.

What to Avoid

Strictly steer clear of raw unpeeled vegetables, unpasteurized milk, spicy oily curries, and stale food.

Satori Mind-Body Immunity: 4-7-8 Rhythmic Breathing

Emotional distress directly influences autonomic nervous tension. Sanjeevani’s Satori protocol introduces evidence-backed pranayama to reduce pre-chemo panic.

Step 1: Exhale completely through your mouth with a gentle whooshing sound.

Step 2: Close your mouth and inhale quietly through your nose to a mental count of 4.

Step 3: Hold your breath calmly for a count of 7.

Step 4: Exhale completely through your mouth to a count of 8. Repeat 4 cycles.

Government Support & Financial Aid Navigation

Through Sanjeevani's CanDaan program, counselors help navigate central and state healthcare welfare schemes:

  • Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY): Up to ₹5 Lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary cancer care.
  • Prime Minister's National Relief Fund (PMNRF): Partial grant assistance for life-threatening illnesses.
  • Health Minister’s Cancer Patient Fund (HMCPF): Financial assistance up to ₹2 Lakhs for patients below poverty line.
