# Sanjeevani AI — CanSupport Affective Computing & RAG Platform

Universal Multimodal Emotion Recognition, Explainable Psycho-Oncology Reasoning, and Grounded CanSupport RAG System.
Developed in partnership with Sanjeevani – Life Beyond Cancer NGO.

### Features
1. **🎙️ Tab 1: Full Conversation Checkup (Session Analyzer)**: Multi-turn diarized affective assessment across PANAS-20.
2. **💬 Tab 2: Single Message & Voice Diagnostic**: Instant prosody and sentiment fusion triage.
3. **🤖 Tab 3: CanSupport AI Chatbot**: Grounded RAG with source citations and emotion-adaptive tone.
4. **🛡️ Tab 4: Counsellor Escalation & Admin Dashboard**: Real-time escalation queue, SLA metrics, and AI-drafted replies.
5. **📋 Tab 5: Clinical SOAP Note & Mood Tracking**: Automated Subjective-Objective-Assessment-Plan clinical progress notes.
6. **🌐 Tab 6: Patient Portal Explorer**: Browse all 14 Sanjeevani programs and regional cancer hospital chapters.
